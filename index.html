Biography & Athletic Journey of Suresh Malli

A balanced fusion of traditional yogic mastery and formal physical education science.

Born on 10th February 2001 in Borda, Bhilwara (Rajasthan), Suresh Malli (Suresh Mali) began his structured sports training in 2014, specializing in gymnastics at the historic Sukhadia Stadium, Bhilwara.

He competed for Manikya Lal Verma (MLV) Govt College, Bhilwara in the All India Gymnastics Championship before transitioning into competitive Yogasana sports. His technique combines rigorous physical education principles with traditional yogic postures.
